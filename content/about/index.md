<h2>About Me</h2>

<p>Hi there, my name is Michiel, a life enthusiast and mental health advocate on a mission to <span class=bold>inspire, motivate</span>, and <span class=bold>break barriers</span> while figuring out what I want to do for a living. Over the years, I’ve tackled smoking, weed, and porn addiction, learning the power of resilience and <span class=bold>self-forgiveness</span>.</p>
<p>This blog is a space where I explore self-help, personal growth, and topics society often hesitates to discuss. It’s my way of documenting the steps I’m taking to become a better version of myself and learning to embrace life with all its <span class=bold>imperfections</span> —and maybe inspire you to do the same.</p>
<p>For years, I struggled with addictions, self-doubt, and a longing for something more. Starting this blog was my way of saying, ‘I’m ready to change.’ I hope that by sharing my journey, I can spark something in you—a moment of reflection, a new perspective, or the courage to take that first step toward change.</p>
<p>I’d love to hear your stories, challenges, and triumphs. Feel free to <span class=bold>reach out</span> via any of the ways listed at the bottom of this page—I’m always up for a conversation.</p>
<p><span class=cursive>This About page represents my current journey. Just like life, it's subject to change as I grow, learn, and navigate this path.</span></p>
<p>Much love, Michiel</p>
