<h1>Weekly AI News Summary</h1>

<h2>What's Really Happening in AI?</h2>

<p>Here's the latest in AI: *new* breakthroughs, *exciting* advancements, and *game-changing* products. Or at least that’s what the press releases say. Let's dive in, shall we?</p>

<p>Meta's AI chatbot app is coming soon, aiming to compete with the big players. Because why browse the internet when you can chat with an AI instead? Questionable innovation or a stroke of genius? You decide.</p>
